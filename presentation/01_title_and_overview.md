## Speaker Script / Talking Points
> "Good morning/afternoon everyone. Today, I am excited to present **ALGOVIZ**, an interactive web-based algorithm and data structure visualizer.
> 
> Anyone who has studied Computer Science knows that grasping how pointers rewire in a linked list, how recursion unfolds in a binary tree, or how distances relax in Dijkstra's algorithm from textbook pseudocode can be frustratingly abstract. 
> 
> ALGOVIZ solves this by replacing passive reading with an active, interactive sandbox. Users don't just watch pre-rendered videos — they create custom graphs, insert tree nodes, manipulate lists, and step through the algorithms forward and backward with line-by-line synchronized code highlighting. Let's look at why this was built and the problem it addresses."
