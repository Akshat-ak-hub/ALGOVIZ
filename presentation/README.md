## How to Present Using `index.html`

Simply double-click or open `index.html` in your browser.

### Keyboard & Navigation Controls
- **Next Slide:** Press `Right Arrow (→)`, `Spacebar`, or `Page Down`
- **Previous Slide:** Press `Left Arrow (←)`, `Backspace`, or `Page Up`
- **Full Screen Mode:** Press `F` (or click the fullscreen button in the top bar)
- **Toggle Speaker Notes:** Press `S` (or click "Notes" in the top bar)
- **First / Last Slide:** Press `Home` / `End`
- **Touch / Mobile:** Swipe left/right or tap bottom controls

### Printing / Exporting to PDF
- To create a PDF of the slides, open `index.html` in Chrome/Edge, press `Ctrl + P`, set Destination to **Save as PDF**, and choose **Landscape** orientation with **Background graphics** enabled.
